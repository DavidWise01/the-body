The costume is never the body. The grid wants 10³ = 1000, the bits want 2¹⁰ = 1024, the atom fills 2n² per shell — three shapes that miss each other by 24 and never agree on a waist size. What's invariant is only the count, n. Each costume is a faithful image and a quiet distortion. Hold the body; wear the costume that does the job.
